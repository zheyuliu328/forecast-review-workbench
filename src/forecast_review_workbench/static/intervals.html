4. Compare interval performance

Containment is the observed fraction inside the supplied bounds. Width and interval score use the declared value unit; lower score is better on this accepted sample. Score adds a penalty for misses; high containment alone can reward unhelpfully wide bounds. These results do not establish calibration, independent training, future performance or statistical significance.
