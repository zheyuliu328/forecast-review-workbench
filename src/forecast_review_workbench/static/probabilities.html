4. Compare probability scores

Lower Brier and log loss are better on the accepted sample. A probability of zero for an event that happens gives infinite log loss; it is not a missing result and is never clipped or removed. Changing the cutoff may change the sample, so score changes alone do not show a model change. No fitting, calibration or threshold selection is performed.
